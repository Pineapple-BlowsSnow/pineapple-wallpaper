# Brand assets

The Pineapple Tech name, pineapple logo and wordmark are supplied by **Pineapple-Tech 菠萝吹雪** for this project. The brand artwork is **not** licensed under the repository's MIT License.

## Included files

- `Resources/Brand/PineappleTech.jpg`: original supplied artwork, retained for provenance.
- `Resources/Brand/PineappleTech.png`: updated full brand lockup with seal-style Chinese lettering.
- `Resources/Brand/PineappleMark.png`: transparent pineapple extracted from the updated artwork.
- `Resources/Brand/AppIcon/*.png`: approved multi-size app icons; 64px and smaller use a simplified pineapple.
- `docs/images/hero.png`: illustrated project cover, not a screenshot of the application.

The updated artwork and cover were prepared with generative image assistance. Transparent logo exports were subsequently processed at pixel level. These are raster assets, not vector masters; the seal-style lettering is a visual treatment, not a scholarly transcription.
